On the cache-line partitioning (B vs C): a ratio at or below
1.00x is the EXPECTED result on this part. The STM32F407 is a
Cortex-M4 with no data cache and single-cycle SRAM, so the load
the shadow indices remove costs about as much as the branch and
the store that replace it. The partitioning is a portability and
multi-core property - it pays on a cached Cortex-M7, where a
falsely-shared line costs a refill - and it is not claimed as an
M4 speed-up. Measuring it here is what establishes that.
